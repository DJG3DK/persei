Privacy

Persei, Persei Chat and perseiagent.ai · last updated 22 October 2026

Persei is run by one person, Danny, from one server. This page says what is collected, why, where it lives and how to get rid of it. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads or third-party trackers anywhere.

Persei Chat (the app and chat.perseiagent.ai)

  • Your account: your email address, a password hash, and your 2FA settings, so you can sign in. Accounts are created by invitation only.
  • Your conversations: the messages you send, the replies, files you attach, and the notes the assistant keeps about past conversations so it can remember them. They are stored on Persei's own server.
  • Answering you: to write a reply, your message and the relevant context are sent to an AI model provider through OpenRouter. Those providers process it to produce the answer under their own terms; Persei does not send it anywhere else.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, your device's push address is stored so a reply can reach you.
  • Server logs: the web server records requests (IP address, time, page) for security and to keep the service running. Logs are rotated and deleted.

perseiagent.ai

  • Downloads: the name and email you give on the download form, the platform, and whether you asked for updates.
  • Newsletter: your name and email, only if you sign up or tick the box. Every issue has an unsubscribe link.
  • Visits: counted from the web server's own logs (no cookies, no analytics script).

The self-hosted agent

If you install Persei yourself, it runs on your machine and keeps its data there. Your code and conversations go only to the model providers you configure. Persei's server does not see them.

Deleting your data

Write to danny@perseiagent.ai and your account, conversations, registration or subscription will be deleted. You can also delete individual conversations in the chat app yourself.

Children

Persei is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes with it.
